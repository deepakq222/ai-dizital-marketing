Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to build a website?

A typical small business website takes two to four weeks, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content is ready.

Will my website work on mobile phones?

Yes. Every website we build is mobile-first and tested on phones and desktops.

Can you redesign my existing website?

Yes. We can improve your current site or rebuild it for better speed, design and enquiries.

What do you need from me to start a website?

Your logo, business details, services, photos if you have them, and examples of websites you like.

Can you add a blog to my website?

Yes. A blog helps you answer customer questions and is one of the best ways to earn search traffic over time.
