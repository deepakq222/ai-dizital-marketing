Website Speed and SEO: How to Make Your Website Load Faster

Web DesignBy AI Dizital Marketing30 Sep 20263 min read

Most visitors open your website on a phone, often on mobile data. If the page takes too long to appear, many leave before they read a single word. Speed affects your leads, your ad costs and your search visibility, so it deserves attention.

Why Website Speed Matters

  • Visitors: people abandon slow pages and rarely come back.
  • Conversions: a faster site makes it easier to call, message or fill a form.
  • SEO: page experience, including Core Web Vitals, is one of the signals Google uses. It won’t beat great content on its own, but it can separate similar pages.
  • Ads: landing page experience affects how your Google Ads perform.

Understand Core Web Vitals

Google’s Core Web Vitals measure real-world experience with three metrics:

  • Largest Contentful Paint (LCP): how quickly the main content appears. Aim for 2.5 seconds or less.
  • Interaction to Next Paint (INP): how quickly the page responds when someone taps or clicks. Aim for 200 milliseconds or less.
  • Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS): how much the layout jumps around while loading. Aim for 0.1 or less.

Check your scores with PageSpeed Insights and the Core Web Vitals report in Google Search Console.

Fix 1: Optimise Images

Large images are the most common cause of slow pages. Resize images to the size they are displayed at, compress them, use modern formats such as WebP, and lazy-load images further down the page.

Fix 2: Trim Scripts, Plugins and Fonts

Every chat widget, tracking script, slider and plugin adds weight. Remove what you don’t use, load non-essential scripts after the main content, and limit yourself to a couple of font families and weights.

Fix 3: Use Good Hosting and Caching

Choose reliable hosting with servers close to your audience. Enable compression (such as Gzip or Brotli) and browser caching so returning visitors load pages faster. A CDN can help if your visitors are spread across regions.

Fix 4: Stop Layout Jumps

Set width and height on images and videos, reserve space for banners and embeds, and avoid inserting pop-ups above existing content. A stable layout feels faster and prevents accidental taps.

Test like a customer: open your website on your own phone using mobile data, not office Wi-Fi. If it feels slow to you, it feels slow to your customers too.

Conclusion

Speed is not a one-time fix. Check your Core Web Vitals regularly, keep images light and avoid adding heavy plugins without a reason. For more ways your site may be losing enquiries, read 9 website design mistakes that cost you leads, or ask us to build a fast, conversion-focused website.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does website speed affect Google rankings?

Yes, as part of page experience signals. It matters most when competing pages have similar content and relevance.

What is a good PageSpeed Insights score?

Focus on passing the Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP and CLS) rather than chasing a perfect score of 100.

What slows down most small business websites?

Oversized images, too many plugins and scripts, heavy fonts and slow hosting are the most common causes.
