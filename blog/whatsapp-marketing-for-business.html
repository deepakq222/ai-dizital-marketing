WhatsApp Marketing for Business: Turn Chats Into Customers

Lead GenerationBy AI Dizital Marketing22 Aug 20263 min read

In India, customers would often rather send a WhatsApp message than fill out a form or make a call. That makes WhatsApp one of the most powerful — and most underused — sales channels for small businesses.

1. Set Up WhatsApp Business Properly

  • Use the free WhatsApp Business app, not your personal account.
  • Add your business name, logo, description, address, hours and website.
  • Create a catalogue of your services or products with prices or starting prices.
  • For higher volumes, consider the WhatsApp Business Platform (API), which supports automation and multiple team members.

2. Save Time With Smart Replies

Set up a greeting message for first-time chats, an away message for after hours, and quick replies for common questions like pricing, location and process. Fast, consistent answers win more customers.

3. Organise Leads With Labels

Use labels such as “New Lead”, “Quote Sent”, “Follow Up” and “Customer”. This simple system turns your chat list into a mini CRM so no enquiry is forgotten.

4. Bring More People Into WhatsApp

  • Add a floating WhatsApp button to your website.
  • Put your click-to-chat link in your Instagram bio and Google Business Profile.
  • Run click-to-WhatsApp ads on Facebook and Instagram.
  • Add a QR code to your shop, visiting cards and flyers.

5. Use Broadcasts and Status Wisely

Broadcast lists only reach people who have saved your number, so always ask customers to save it. Share genuinely useful updates, offers and tips — not daily spam. WhatsApp Status is a free way to share work samples and testimonials with everyone who saved your number.

Golden rule: Only message people who have opted in, and always make it easy to stop receiving messages. Trust is your most valuable asset.

6. Close the Sale in Chat

Respond quickly, ask one or two qualifying questions, share a relevant example of your work and suggest a clear next step — a call, a visit or a payment link. The faster and more personal your replies, the higher your conversion rate.

Conclusion

WhatsApp turns marketing into conversation, and conversation turns into sales. Set it up properly, respond fast and follow up consistently. Want WhatsApp automation set up for your business? Talk to us.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is WhatsApp Business free?

Yes, the WhatsApp Business app is free. The WhatsApp Business Platform (API) charges per conversation based on Meta’s pricing.

Can I send bulk messages on WhatsApp?

Bulk promotional messages should be sent through the official Business Platform to opted-in users. Unsolicited bulk messages can get your number banned.

How many contacts can a broadcast list have?

Broadcast lists in the WhatsApp Business app have a recipient limit, and messages only reach contacts who have saved your number.
