Google Business Profile Optimisation: 12 Ways to Get More Calls From Google Maps

Local SEOBy AI Dizital Marketing6 Oct 20263 min read

For local businesses, the Google Business Profile is often the first thing customers see — before your website, before your Instagram. A complete, active profile gets more calls, more direction requests and more trust. Here are 12 practical ways to improve yours.

Get the Basics Right (1–4)

  1. Choose the most accurate primary category. It has a big influence on which searches you appear for. Add a few relevant secondary categories too.
  2. Use your real business name. Adding keywords or city names to the name breaks Google’s guidelines and can lead to suspension.
  3. Keep name, address and phone consistent with your website and other listings, in exactly the same format.
  4. Set accurate hours, including special hours for holidays, so customers never find you closed when Google says open.

Fill Every Section (5–8)

  1. Write a helpful description (up to 750 characters) explaining what you do, who you help and where you work, in natural language.
  2. Add every service or product with a short description. This helps Google match you to more specific searches.
  3. Upload real photos regularly: your storefront, interior, team and finished work. Real photos build trust far better than stock images.
  4. Add attributes that apply to you, such as wheelchair access, online appointments or payment options.

Build Trust With Reviews (9–10)

  1. Ask every customer for a review. Share your review link on WhatsApp right after a job is done. Never buy reviews or offer rewards for them.
  2. Reply to every review, positive or negative. A calm, helpful reply to a bad review often impresses future customers more than a perfect score.

Stay Active (11–12)

  1. Publish updates about offers, new work, events or tips. Regular posts show customers and Google that the business is active.
  2. Check Performance insights monthly to see calls, website clicks, direction requests and the searches people used to find you, then improve what is weak.
Quick check: search your business name and main service on Google Maps from your phone. Look at your profile as a customer would. Anything missing, outdated or unclear is costing you calls.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Creating duplicate profiles for the same location.
  • Using a virtual office address you don’t actually operate from.
  • Ignoring the profile for months after setting it up.
  • Changing your business name or category frequently.

Conclusion

A strong Google Business Profile is one of the most cost-effective marketing assets a local business can have. Fill it completely, collect genuine reviews and keep it active every month. For the bigger local SEO picture, read our local SEO guide, or let our team optimise your profile for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I post on Google Business Profile?

Posting about once a week is a good, sustainable rhythm. Consistency matters more than volume.

Do photos really help my Google Maps ranking?

Photos mainly help customers choose you. Profiles with real, recent photos usually get more clicks, calls and direction requests.

Can I have a Google Business Profile without a shop?

Yes. Service-area businesses can hide their address and list the areas they serve instead.
