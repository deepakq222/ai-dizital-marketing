How to Choose a Digital Marketing Agency in Delhi: 10 Questions to Ask

StrategyBy AI Dizital Marketing9 Oct 20264 min read

Delhi has hundreds of digital marketing agencies, and most of them promise more leads, higher rankings and better ROI. The hard part is telling a good partner from a costly mistake before you sign. These ten questions help you compare agencies on what actually matters: honesty, process, ownership and results you can check.

1. Do They Ask About Your Business First?

A good agency asks about your customers, your services, your margins and your goals before talking about packages. If the first call is a price list, the plan will probably be generic. Look for questions like “Which services bring you the most profit?” and “Where do your best customers come from today?”

2. Do They Guarantee Rankings?

No one controls Google’s rankings, so no honest agency can guarantee a #1 position. Promises like “page one in 30 days” are a warning sign. A trustworthy agency explains what it will do, how long SEO usually takes and how progress will be measured.

3. Can They Show Relevant Work?

Ask for examples from businesses similar to yours, such as local service businesses, clinics, shops or coaching institutes. Good examples explain the starting point, what was done and what changed. If possible, ask to speak with a current client.

4. Who Will Actually Do the Work?

Find out who will manage your account, how experienced they are and whether any work is outsourced. You should have one clear point of contact who understands your business.

5. How Will You Measure Success?

Likes and impressions are easy to grow but don’t pay bills. Agree on business numbers such as calls, form enquiries, WhatsApp messages, bookings and cost per lead. Ask to see a sample monthly report before you start.

Tip: Ask the agency to set up call and form tracking in the first month. Without tracking, nobody can tell which marketing is working.

6. Who Owns the Accounts and the Website?

Your Google Ads account, Meta Business account, Google Business Profile, website, domain and analytics should be in your name, with the agency added as a user. If you part ways, you keep your data, history and assets.

7. How Is the Ad Budget Handled?

Ad spend should be paid directly to Google or Meta from your own account, separate from the agency’s fee. This keeps spending transparent and lets you check every rupee in the platform itself.

8. What Happens in the First 90 Days?

Ask for a simple plan: what gets fixed or set up in month one, what gets launched in month two and what gets improved in month three. A clear plan shows the agency has thought about your business, not just its package.

9. Are Their Methods Safe?

Fake reviews, bought followers, spammy backlinks and keyword stuffing can bring short-term numbers and long-term penalties. Ask how they build links, how they get reviews and how they grow social accounts. The answers should sound boring and honest.

10. What Are the Contract Terms?

Check the minimum period, notice period, what is included each month and what costs extra. Make sure the scope is written down, so both sides know exactly what to expect.

Quick Red Flags

  • Guaranteed rankings or a guaranteed number of leads
  • Very cheap packages with long lists of vague deliverables
  • Refusing to give you access to your own accounts
  • Reports full of jargon but no calls or enquiries
  • Pressure to sign today

Conclusion

The right agency is clear about goals, honest about timelines, transparent about money and happy for you to own everything. Use these questions with every agency you speak to, including us. To see how we work, read about our digital marketing services or talk to our team in Madangir, New Delhi.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a digital marketing agency in Delhi charge?

It depends on the services, competition and scope of work. Ask for a written proposal that lists the monthly fee, what is included and any extra costs, and keep ad spend separate from the agency fee.

How long should I give an agency before judging results?

Paid ads can show early results within weeks. SEO and Local SEO usually need three to six months of consistent work before you can judge them fairly.

Should I hire a local agency or a remote one?

Either can work. A local agency in Delhi can meet you in person and may know local search behaviour better, but process, honesty and communication matter more than distance.
