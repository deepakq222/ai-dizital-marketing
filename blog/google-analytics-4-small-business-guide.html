Google Analytics 4 for Small Businesses: What to Track and Why

StrategyBy AI Dizital Marketing9 Oct 20263 min read

Google Analytics 4 (GA4) shows who visits your website, where they come from and what they do. Most small businesses don’t need every report. They need to know which marketing brings enquiries. This guide covers just that.

1. Set Up GA4 Properly

  • Create a GA4 property in your own Google account, so you always own the data.
  • Add a web data stream for your website and copy the Measurement ID (it starts with G-).
  • Add the Google tag to every page of your site, or install it through Google Tag Manager.
  • Open the Realtime report and visit your site to confirm data is arriving.

2. Change Two Settings on Day One

  • Data retention: the default for detailed reports is 2 months. Change it to 14 months in Admin → Data settings so you can compare longer periods.
  • Enhanced measurement: keep it on. It automatically tracks page views, scrolls, outbound clicks, site search, video engagement, file downloads and form interactions.

3. Track the Actions That Mean Business

A visit is not a lead. Set up events for the actions that show real interest, then mark them as key events (Google’s current name for conversions):

  • Phone clicks: clicks on tel: links
  • WhatsApp clicks: clicks on wa.me links
  • Form submissions: successful contact form sends
  • Bookings or purchases, if your website takes them
Tip: Name events clearly, such as call_click, whatsapp_click and form_submit, so reports are easy to read months later.

4. The Reports Worth Checking

  • Traffic acquisition: which channels bring visitors and key events, such as Organic Search, Paid Search, Direct and Organic Social.
  • Pages and screens: which pages get the most views and engagement.
  • Landing page: which entry pages lead to enquiries.
  • Demographics and Tech: visitor cities and the share of mobile users.

5. Tag Your Campaign Links

Add UTM parameters to links you share in WhatsApp broadcasts, emails, Instagram bios and QR codes. For example, ?utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=broadcast&utm_campaign=diwali_offer. Without UTMs, many of these visits show up as Direct and you can’t tell what worked.

6. Connect Your Other Google Tools

Link GA4 with Google Ads to see which campaigns bring key events, and with Google Search Console to see search queries next to on-site behaviour.

7. Common Mistakes

  • Letting an agency or developer own the GA4 property
  • Installing the tag twice, which doubles page views
  • Tracking only page views and never setting up key events
  • Never checking the data after setup

Conclusion

GA4 becomes useful the moment it tracks calls, WhatsApp clicks and form enquiries. Set it up in your own account, track the actions that matter and review a few reports each month. Need help connecting analytics to your ads and SEO? Explore our Google Ads management or contact our team.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Google Analytics 4 free?

Yes. The standard version of GA4 is free and is enough for almost every small business.

What is a key event in GA4?

A key event is an action you mark as important, such as a form submission or phone click. It replaced the older “conversion” label in GA4 reports.

Can GA4 track phone calls?

GA4 can track clicks on your phone number link on the website. Calls made by typing the number manually need a call-tracking tool.
