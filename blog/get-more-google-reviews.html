How to Get More Google Reviews (The Right Way)

Local SEOBy AI Dizital Marketing8 Oct 20263 min read

Reviews are often the deciding factor when someone compares businesses on Google Maps. More genuine, recent reviews build trust, help your profile stand out and support your local rankings. The good news: most happy customers will leave a review — if you ask at the right moment and make it easy.

Why Google Reviews Matter

  • Trust: people read reviews before calling, visiting or booking.
  • Visibility: review count, rating and recency are part of how Google evaluates local businesses.
  • Feedback: reviews tell you what customers love and what to fix.

1. Ask at the Right Moment

The best time to ask is right after a customer has experienced the value of your work — when the job is finished, the order is delivered or they thank you. A request sent days later is easy to forget.

2. Make It One Tap

Copy your review link from your Google Business Profile and share it directly. Customers should land straight on the review box, not have to search for your business.

  • Send it on WhatsApp with a short, personal message.
  • Add it to invoices, receipts and email signatures.
  • Print a QR code for your counter or reception.

3. Use a Simple Message

Example: “Thank you for choosing us, Rahul! If you were happy with our work, could you share a quick review on Google? It really helps a small business like ours. Here’s the link: [your link]”

Keep it short, personal and polite. Mention their name and the specific work you did.

4. Follow Google’s Rules

Google removes reviews that break its policies, and violations can hurt your profile. Avoid these:

  • Offering rewards such as discounts or gifts in exchange for reviews.
  • Asking only happy customers while filtering out others. Ask everyone in the same way.
  • Writing fake reviews or asking staff, friends or family to review as customers.
  • Buying reviews from any service.

5. Reply to Every Review

Replies show future customers that you care. Thank people for positive reviews and mention the service they used. For negative reviews, stay calm, apologise for the experience, avoid arguing in public and offer to resolve it by phone or message. A thoughtful reply to a bad review can win more trust than a perfect rating.

6. Make It a Habit

A steady flow of reviews looks more natural and keeps your profile fresh. Build asking for reviews into your normal process, so every completed job ends with a polite request.

Conclusion

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I offer a discount in exchange for a Google review?

No. Google’s policies don’t allow incentives for reviews, and such reviews can be removed.

Can I delete a negative Google review?

You can’t delete it yourself. You can reply professionally, and you can report it to Google only if it breaks their policies, such as spam or offensive content.

How many reviews does a business need?

There’s no fixed number. Aim for a steady flow of genuine, recent reviews and compare yourself with the businesses ranking near you.
