Meta Ads for Small Businesses: A Beginner’s Guide to Facebook & Instagram Ads

Social MediaBy AI Dizital Marketing8 Oct 20264 min read

Facebook and Instagram ads (now run together as Meta ads) let a small business reach people in a specific city, age group or interest with a budget it controls. Used well, they bring enquiries every day. Used carelessly, they burn money on likes that never become customers.

This guide explains how to set up Meta ads the right way, even if you have never run one before.

1. Pick the Right Campaign Objective

Meta’s Ads Manager asks you to choose an objective first, and it optimises delivery for that goal. The six objectives are Awareness, Traffic, Engagement, Leads, App Promotion and Sales. For most local service businesses:

  • Leads is the best starting point. You can collect enquiries with an instant form, on WhatsApp or on your website.
  • Engagement → messages works well when customers prefer to chat on WhatsApp, Messenger or Instagram DMs before buying.
  • Sales suits online stores that have tracking set up on the website.

Avoid choosing Engagement for likes or Awareness when what you really want is enquiries. Meta will deliver exactly what you ask for.

2. Target the People Who Can Actually Buy

  • Location: set the cities or radius you actually serve. A Delhi salon gains nothing from clicks in Chennai.
  • Age and language: match your real customer base.
  • Interests or broad targeting: start broad within your location and let Meta’s delivery system find the people most likely to respond. Narrow down only if results are poor.
  • Retargeting: show ads to people who visited your website or engaged with your Instagram, because they already know you.

3. Create Ads That Stop the Scroll

Your creative (image or video) matters more than any setting. People scroll fast, so your ad has a second or two to earn attention.

  • Use real photos or short videos of your work, team or results instead of generic stock images.
  • Put the main message in the first line of text and the first seconds of video.
  • Show the offer clearly: what they get, for whom, and why now.
  • Use vertical formats for Reels and Stories, where a lot of attention is today.
  • Test 2–3 different creatives at a time and keep the winners.

4. Make It Easy to Become a Lead

Whatever objective you choose, the next step must be simple. Keep instant forms short (name, phone and one qualifying question), reply to WhatsApp messages quickly, and if you send people to your website, send them to a focused landing page, not the homepage.

Speed wins: leads go cold quickly. Reply to every new enquiry as fast as you can, ideally within minutes during business hours.

5. Track Results Properly

Install the Meta Pixel on your website and, if possible, the Conversions API, so Meta knows which ads lead to real enquiries and purchases. Without tracking, the system optimises blindly and you cannot tell which ads make money.

Judge ads by cost per lead and cost per customer, not by likes, reach or clicks alone.

6. Give the Campaign Time, Then Optimise

New ad sets go through a learning phase while Meta figures out who to show them to. Meta generally needs around 50 results in a week for an ad set to exit learning, so avoid changing budgets and targeting every day. After the first week or two, pause ads with a high cost per lead, add budget to the winners and test fresh creatives regularly.

Conclusion

Meta ads work best when the objective matches your goal, targeting matches your real customers and every lead gets a fast reply. Start small, track everything and scale what works. Want experts to run your campaigns? See our social media and paid ads services.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Facebook ads still worth it for small businesses?

Yes. With tight location targeting, a clear offer and fast follow-up, Meta ads remain one of the most affordable ways for local businesses to get enquiries.

Should I boost posts or use Ads Manager?

Ads Manager is better for getting leads. It gives you more control over objectives, targeting, placements and tracking than the Boost button.

How long before Meta ads start working?

You can see first results within days, but most campaigns need one to two weeks of data before they can be judged and optimised properly.
